# Author support

The app links directly to https://boosty.to/overyx/donate and TikTok @overyx2.
It handles no card data and charges no application fee. Capri remains free.

Boosty's [official FAQ](https://boosty.to/boosty/posts/de1e6f32-3a80-4824-95bd-a264ff1b00aa)
describes Russian-card payouts in RUB and foreign-card payments, with mandatory
author verification. It also explains that its payment partner can decline a
foreign card. Therefore the app must not promise payment acceptance from every
country/card or guaranteed payouts. The owner configures payout details and
verification directly in Boosty. Review the service's current fees and eligibility
there before announcing payment availability to an audience.
